How we research lived data

We study how people understand, feel, and experience data through narrative, affective, and embodied approaches.

Our perspective on lived data has been shaped by years of collaboration with people with intellectual and developmental disabilities (IDD). These partnerships challenged conventional assumptions about how data should be represented, interpreted, and experienced, and continue to shape how we think about visualization.
