The Lived Data Collective

  • Data Visualization
  • Accessibility
  • Neurodiversity
  • Human–Computer Interaction

We are a research group at the University of Maryland, College Park, led by Dr. Keke Wu. We study how visualization can reconnect data with human experience. We design new representations and interactive systems that open up alternative ways for people to relate to data, maing it more accessible, expressive and meaningful. We believe that grounding data in people's lives and communities can foster understanding, reflection, and action, and help build a more inclusive data-driven society.
