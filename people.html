Who makes the collective

A growing interdisciplinary community exploring how data can become more accessible, expressive, and meaningful in everyday life.

Lab Members

Dr. Keke Wu

Principal Investigator

Visualization · Accessibility · Storytelling

Keke is an Assistant Professor and director of the collective. Her research explores how visualization can broaden the ways people understand, experience, and make meaning from data, with a focus on cognitive accessibility, storytelling, and creative technology. Her work draws on community-engaged and arts-informed approaches.

Maggie Xu

Graduate Researcher (PhD)

Accessibility · Aging · Cognition

Collaborating with Dr. Amanda Lazar. Maggie is an Info Science PhD student with a background in psychology and product design. She is interested in accessibility, particularly in working alongside older adults and people with intellectual or developmental disabilities to inform more supportive design for people with different learning and processing needs.

Nony Khondaker

Graduate Researcher (MS)

Visualization · Game Design

Nony is an international graduate student from Bangladesh currently studying HCI at UMD. He has 4+ years of experience in UX/UI and 10+ years in visual design. His research interests include data visualization, accessibility and game design.

Trisha Jambhale

Graduate Researcher (MS)

UI/UX · Data Storytelling

Trisha is an HCIM student from India at UMD. Her UI/UX design background shapes how she approaches research, and she is currently contributing to the Data Storytelling project in the Lived Data Collective. Her broader interests lie at the intersection of AI and user behaviour.

Kai-Ting Chiu

Graduate Researcher (MS)

Personal Informatics · Data Visualization

Kai Ting is an HCI master's student with a background in psychology, UX design, and interactive art. Her research focuses on design challenges in personal informatics, with a particular interest in women's bodily experiences. She explores how aesthetic and embodied data visualization can help people reflect on their personal data and develop deeper self-awareness.

Diego Abarcar-Calugay

Graduate Researcher (MS)

Accessibility · Storytelling · Creative Technology

Diego is a Computer Science major interested in exploring the intersection between technology, design, and storytelling. He is passionate about investigating how this intersection can be used to promote accessibility and good mental health.

Isabella Amador

Undergraduate Researcher

Inclusive Design · Mental Health

Isabella is an Information Design and Psychology double major with interests in UI, UX, inclusive design, and digital accessibility. She is interested in investigating the ways technology can increase access to mental health services and improve psychological well-being.
