Prospective PhD Students

We’re looking for 1–2 PhD students for Fall 2027 to rethink how people understand, feel , and experience data, with particular interest in:

  • Narrative Data: Using storytelling to improve data accessibility or connect data with lived experience.
  • Affective Data: Representing emotion and felt experience beyond labels and measures.
  • Embodied Data: Experiencing data through bodies, spaces, and immersive interaction.

We welcome ideas that challenge conventional ways of representing and engaging with data.
