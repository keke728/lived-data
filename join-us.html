Prospective PhD Students

We’re looking for 1–2 PhD students for Fall 2027 to rethink how people relate to data. Possible directions include:

  • Narrative Data: Connecting data to human experiences and social issues through storytelling.
  • Affective Data: Representing subjective and felt experiences beyond labels and metrics.
  • Embodied Data: Exploring how people experience and express data through XR and the body.

We welcome your ideas and perspectives, including directions we haven't imagined.
